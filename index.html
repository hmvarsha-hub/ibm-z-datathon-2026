WHY IT HELPS

What it is used for

  • Balance green time across high and low density approaches.
  • Estimate wait and congestion by junction road.
  • Prepare a priority corridor from authenticated ambulance telemetry.
  • Show operators a 30-second refresh and a decision trail.

How it differs from common fixed-cycle approaches

  • Adjusts a recommended green plan for each approach based on density tiers.
  • Compares all mapped road approaches instead of displaying a single junction score.
  • Combines traffic estimates and a fleet-authenticated emergency workflow in one pilot view.
  • Designed to connect with authorized sensors and fleet systems; this demo uses simulated values.
